# Executive summary report template

The data-backed report is generated as `outputs/summary.md` after `python analysis.py` runs against the locally downloaded `train.csv`. Use that report as the basis for the required two-page PDF or DOCX, and verify its numbers and recommendations with a human reviewer. Do not submit invented values. The analysis pipeline reports historical findings and forecast estimates, but cannot infer causal explanations for anomalies without promotion, stockout, pricing, and operational data.
